Biography

I am a Ph.D. student in the School of Integrated Circuits at Peking University, advised by Prof. Bonan Yan, and expect to graduate in June 2027. I received my B.E. in Microelectronics Science and Engineering from Peking University. My research focuses on computer architecture and dataflow design, particularly the coordinated organization of computation, memory, and communication for efficient and scalable intelligent systems. My work spans memory-centric distributed architectures, analog–digital heterogeneous dataflows, and reliability techniques for processing-in-memory systems.
